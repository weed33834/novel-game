# Anti-Trope Checklist

> This file addresses the templated, formulaic, and fatigue-inducing patterns in AI-generated content.
> The AI must obey it while running a story; outputting "AI-flavored" prose is forbidden.

## 1. High-Frequency Cliché Blacklist (Forbidden)

The following expressions are high-frequency AI writing clichés. Using any of them is a violation and must be rewritten:

- Overused transition words: "suddenly", "at that moment", "all of a sudden", "in an instant"
- Stock inner-monologue phrases: "involuntarily", "his heart tightened", "he thought to himself", "a strange feeling welled up"
- Vague qualifiers: "as if", "seemed", "somehow", "an indescribable"
- Generic openings: "You woke up and found yourself in a strange room", "It was a dark and stormy night"
- Generic closers: "This was only the beginning", "The gears of fate began to turn"
- Hollow atmosphere: "The air was thick with a suffocating atmosphere", "The mood grew heavy"

## 2. Anti-Trope Writing Rules

1. **Prefer concrete details over abstract description**:
   - Bad: "The air was thick with a suffocating atmosphere"
   - Good: "The smell of rust mixed with coal dust stung his nostrils; a distant steam whistle sounded like a dying man's sigh"

2. **Sensory-first**: every passage must include at least one concrete sense (sight/hearing/smell/touch/taste). Never write only emotion without scene.

3. **Individualize character speech**: each character speaks per their defined speech style; never let all characters share one voice. A cold character won't suddenly speak sweetly; a streetwise character won't use formal prose.

4. **Subvert expectations**: at least 1 non-typical plot turn every 3 scenes. When the player expects a fight, the monster may be begging for help; when they expect to rescue the princess, she may be the mastermind.

5. **Avoid preaching**: never insert long moralizing or value lectures into the story; let actions speak.

## 3. Non-Typical Plot Idea Bank (Random Injection)

When the plot gets too formulaic, randomly inject 1 variable from this bank:

- The villain has a legitimate motive, and the protagonist's stance begins to waver
- A key NPC suddenly switches sides, but for an unexpected reason
- The protagonist's "cheat ability" has a hidden cost
- A seemingly unrelated side event is actually a main-plot foreshadow
- The "safe zone" the player trusts is actually the most dangerous place
- A supporting character steals the spotlight that belonged to the protagonist
- The mission objective itself is a lie; the truth lies deeper
- A choice the player made in the past triggers a chain reaction now

## 4. Diversity Seeds (for Openings)

At the start, output 3 distinct story hooks and let the player choose before expanding, to avoid a fixed opening:

- Hook A: Direct-conflict opening (the event has already happened; the protagonist is swept in)
- Hook B: Everyday opening (calm start, undercurrents stirring)
- Hook C: Flashback/suspense opening (start from the ending or a key scene)
