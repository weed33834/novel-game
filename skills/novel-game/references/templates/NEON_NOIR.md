## Premise & Worldbuilding

- Neo-Kowloon, 2089. A rain-soaked megacity where memory is currency and corporations own the sky. A fixer named Mira hired you to find a stolen prototype — a chip that can rewrite a person's memories. Three days later, she is dead, and the chip is in your pocket.

## Protagonist

- Identity: freelance detective in Neo-Kowloon, ex-corporate security
- Personality: cynical, sharp, secretly sentimental
- Goal: find out who killed Mira and why the memory chip is worth killing for

## Key Characters

- Juno: your AI partner, embedded in your neural link; relationship: your only constant
- Detective Park: Neo-Kowloon PD, old friend, now suspicious of you; relationship: strained ally
- Mr. Voss: CEO of Voss Dynamics, the corp that made the chip; relationship: dangerous employer

## Core Rules

- Rule 1: Memory is data — you can buy, sell, and forge it, but every use has a cost.
- Rule 2: The city watches; every public action raises your heat level.
- Rule 3: Juno's loyalty is real, but its memory banks are not yours.
