# DRAGON ACADEMY — A Fantasy School Story

## Language

- Output language: en (English). Switch to zh/ja anytime in-session.

## Premise & Worldbuilding

- The Emberfall Academy of Magic has stood for a thousand years, training the realm's mages. This term, a sealed vault beneath the library has begun leaking a black mist, and students are vanishing one by one. You are a first-year student who can hear whispers from the vault.

## Protagonist

- Identity: first-year student at Emberfall Academy, late-bloomer mage
- Personality: observant, stubborn, secretly afraid of your own power
- Goal: find the vanished students before the Academy's midwinter festival — or become the next to disappear

## Key Characters

- Professor Ashworth: stern rune-master, knows more about the vault than he admits; relationship: your mentor
- Lyra: your roommate, a prodigy duelist with a hidden past; relationship: your closest friend
- Kael: a senior student and prefect, suspiciously interested in the disappearances; relationship: ambiguous ally

## Core Rules

- Rule 1: Magic has a cost — every spell you cast drains stamina or leaves a mark.
- Rule 2: The black mist feeds on fear; staying calm is a real mechanic, not flavor.
- Rule 3: The vault whispers only to you; telling the wrong person gets you silenced.

## Lorebook Entries (trigger keyword → setting content)

- The Vault: a sealed chamber under the library, built to contain the "First Flame" that burned the old kingdom
- Black Mist: a living curse that erases memories of those it takes
- Midwinter Festival: the Academy's grand celebration, three days away
- First Flame: the ancient fire that founded the Academy; its true nature is forbidden knowledge
