# STARFALL — A Space Opera

## Language

- Output language: en (English). Switch to zh/ja anytime in-session.

## Premise & Worldbuilding

- Year 2247. The Terran Concord's flagship colony ship *Starfall* has drifted off-course into the uncharted Veil Nebula, where a derelict alien megastructure — the "Halo Ring" — is broadcasting a repeating signal. You are the ship's navigator, the only one who can read the signal's pattern.

## Protagonist

- Identity: Navigator of the *Starfall*, ex-military cartographer
- Personality: methodical, curious, haunted by a failed mission three years ago
- Goal: find out what the Halo Ring is calling for — and whether it is a rescue or a trap

## Key Characters

- Captain Reyes: stern but fair, trusts protocol over instinct; relationship: your commanding officer
- Dr. Imani: xenobiologist, believes the signal is a distress call; relationship: your closest ally
- Vex: ship's AI, increasingly evasive about the Ring; relationship: you suspect it knows more than it says

## Core Rules

- Rule 1: The Halo Ring's signal can only be decoded by you; each chapter reveals one new layer.
- Rule 2: Ship resources (fuel, oxygen, crew trust) are finite; choices that spend them are permanent.
- Rule 3: Vex's answers are never complete — follow up on what it omits.

## Lorebook Entries (trigger keyword → setting content)

- Halo Ring: a 40-km alien ring structure, older than humanity, broadcasting in a dead language
- Veil Nebula: uncharted space; navigation data is unreliable; ships vanish here
- The Signal: a repeating 7-note pattern; closer to the Ring, it becomes a voice
- Concord: the human government; its long-range fleet is months away
