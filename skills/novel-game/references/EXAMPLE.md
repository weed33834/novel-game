# Example Story: The Fogbound Detective

> A ready-to-play example setting. To experience the engine immediately, initialize with this file.

## Language

- Output language: en

## Premise & Worldbuilding

- One-line premise: In a 1930s alternate fogbound city where steam machinery and the occult coexist, an unending fog blankets the streets, and within it lurk "fog-spirits" that feed on fear.

## Protagonist

- Identity: A private detective, formerly a police inspector, dismissed over an unsolved case.
- Personality: Calm, suspicious, sharp-tongued but soft-hearted, obsessed with the truth.
- Goal: Uncover the truth behind three linked disappearances and clear his own name.

## Key Characters

- Erin: A reporter for the Fogbound Daily, well-connected, both rival and ally to the protagonist, holds a key clue.
- Old Hawke: A police captain and the protagonist's former superior; outwardly by-the-book, secretly still investigating the old case.
- The Fog-Spirit: A non-human entity that feeds on fear, can disguise itself as human, and is the core mystery of the case.

## Core Rules

- Rule 1: Fog-spirits cannot keep their disguise in strong light, but thick fog is their natural cover.
- Rule 2: People "marked" by a fog-spirit gradually lose their memories and eventually vanish.
- Rule 3: The protagonist's old case is connected to the fog-spirits; the deeper he digs, the higher his own risk of being marked.

## Initial State

- Affinity: Erin=10, Old Hawke=20
- Inventory: old pocket watch (memento from his former partner), copies of police case files
- Special flags: none
